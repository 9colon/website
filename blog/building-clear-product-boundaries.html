Why clear product boundaries make platforms easier to grow

A practical look at keeping identity shared while product data and business rules remain independently owned.

9

9Colon Engineering

Technology team · October 1, 2026

Shared identity should reduce friction for customers. It should not force every product into one tightly coupled application.

Share stable contracts

Identity, tenant context, and product entitlements belong to a central authority. Each product still owns its business rules, permissions, data, and operations.

Keep deployment choices open

Clear boundaries let teams evolve products independently while preserving a coherent customer experience.
