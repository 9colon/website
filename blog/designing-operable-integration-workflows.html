IntegrationEngineering

Designing integration workflows teams can actually operate

Why validation, retries, ownership, and observable execution should be designed into an integration from the beginning.

9

9Colon Engineering

Technology team · October 8, 2026

Moving data is only one part of an integration. The harder question is whether a team can understand and recover the workflow when something changes.

Design the operating path

Define validation, retry behavior, ownership, and failure visibility alongside the successful path. These decisions determine whether an integration remains dependable after launch.

Make context visible

Useful operational records connect an execution to its tenant, integration, flow, job, and synchronization context. That makes investigation faster without hiding important boundaries.
