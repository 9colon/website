Frequently asked questions

Commerce questions, answered.

Practical answers about platforms, deployment models and how engagements are scoped.

What is the difference between Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce?

Magento Open Source is the free, self-hosted commerce platform you deploy and operate yourself. Adobe Commerce is the commercial edition with additional enterprise capabilities, support and deployment options. Both share a common codebase, so engineering skills transfer, but licensing, hosting and some features differ.

Do you develop and support existing Magento 2 websites?

Yes. We work on existing Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce codebases, including custom modules, themes, upgrades, performance work, integrations and ongoing maintenance.

Which Adobe Commerce deployment models do you support?

We work across Adobe Commerce on-premises, Adobe Commerce on cloud infrastructure (PaaS) and Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service (SaaS). The right model depends on your requirements, entitlements and operating constraints, which we confirm before scoping.

Can you modernize Magento without a complete replatform?

Often, yes. Storefront modernization, catalog and discovery improvements and integration work can be delivered around the existing commerce core. We assess what should be retained before recommending replacement.

What is Adobe Edge Delivery Services?

Edge Delivery Services is Adobe’s approach to fast, content-driven storefronts, using a commerce boilerplate, drop-in components and custom content blocks. Applicability depends on your platform, deployment model and entitlements, which we confirm before scoping.

When should a business consider Adobe Commerce Optimizer?

Commerce Optimizer is relevant when you want composable catalog and discovery capabilities, catalog views and policies, or storefront modernization without replacing an existing commerce backend. Adobe documents it as a SaaS-only offering for Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service and Adobe Commerce Optimizer projects, so we confirm eligibility and entitlements before scoping.

Do you develop Adobe App Builder applications?

Yes. We build custom App Builder applications and out-of-process extensions using Adobe I/O Runtime, Events and API Mesh where those capabilities are supported for your platform.

Do you support enterprise B2B commerce?

Yes. We work on B2B and wholesale requirements including company accounts, catalog and pricing rules, quotes, approvals and distributor portals, scoped to the capabilities of the selected platform and license.

Do you develop custom Shopify and BigCommerce applications?

Yes. We build custom themes, storefronts and applications for Shopify, Shopify Plus and BigCommerce, including headless experiences, migrations and third-party integrations.

Can you integrate commerce platforms with existing enterprise systems?

Yes. We connect commerce platforms with ERP, CRM, PIM, OMS and other systems using supported APIs and event patterns, with explicit contracts and observable execution context.
