Magento and Adobe Commerce, from core platform to composable services

9Colon positions Magento 2 and Adobe Commerce engineering as its primary commerce specialization. We support Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce projects across implementation, modernization, integration, and ongoing engineering. We also work with the newer Adobe Commerce ecosystem where it fits the business and technical context.

These offerings are related, but not interchangeable. Magento Open Source is the open-source edition of the Magento platform. Adobe Commerce is the commercial commerce product with separately licensed capabilities and deployment choices. Adobe Commerce on cloud infrastructure is Adobe-managed platform infrastructure (PaaS); on-premises deployments are operated in customer-managed environments. Adobe Commerce as a Cloud Service is a distinct SaaS offering. A project starts by establishing the product, deployment model, entitlements, existing customizations, and operational ownership—not by assuming every capability applies to every installation.

A grounded modernization approach

We distinguish a platform upgrade from a storefront refresh, a B2B capability extension, a headless or composable architecture, and a move to a SaaS service. Each has different constraints for data, checkout, extensions, deployment, licensing, and operations. Our work can include Magento performance optimization, Adobe Commerce integration services, catalog and order data flows, ERP/CRM/PIM/OMS connectivity, and modernization of existing customizations.

For platform integrations and workflow orchestration, discuss your commerce integration requirements or explore Connectvia, our integration and synchronization product.
