Fast, Content-Led Commerce Storefronts

Commerce storefronts with Adobe Edge Delivery Services

Adobe's developer documentation describes Edge Delivery Services storefront development using its commerce storefront tooling, boilerplate, Commerce drop-ins, and content blocks. We help teams assess and build content-led commerce experiences using the components and integration patterns supported by their selected Adobe products.

A storefront modernization is not automatically a backend migration. The implementation must establish how product data, search, cart, checkout, customer identity, content authoring, and analytics connect to the chosen commerce services.

Fit with your commerce platform

Edge Delivery Services is a storefront and delivery approach, not a synonym for Magento Open Source, Adobe Commerce, Commerce Optimizer, or App Builder. We clarify the backend and service boundaries before recommending components. If the goal is specifically catalog ingestion and discovery while retaining an existing backend, also review Adobe Commerce Optimizer. For platform and deployment choices, see Adobe Commerce development.

Adobe reference

Adobe Commerce Developer Documentation documents the Edge Delivery Services storefront, Commerce drop-ins, boilerplate, content blocks, and the Commerce API surface. Feature support depends on the relevant product, release, and project setup.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about this service

Is Edge Delivery Services a replacement for Adobe Commerce?

No. It is a storefront and delivery approach. The commerce backend, catalog, cart and checkout remain with the commerce services you already run or select.

Does adopting it require a backend migration?

Not necessarily. A storefront modernization can be delivered around an existing commerce core, provided the API boundaries and content workflow are established first.

What are Commerce drop-ins?

Supported storefront components for commerce experiences such as cart and checkout. We assess and integrate the ones that apply, then validate behaviour and accessibility.

Can we keep our current authoring workflow?

Content authoring is part of the design. We map the existing workflow and confirm how merchandising content and commerce data work together before implementation.
