Magento & Adobe Commerce

Magento 2 Development

Magento 2 development services for Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce stores—from custom modules and storefronts to upgrades, performance, and integrations.

Discuss Your Adobe Commerce Project

Magento development for real operating constraints

Magento 2 development spans application code, storefront behavior, catalog and order data, integrations, deployment, and support. 9Colon works on Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce implementations, beginning with an assessment of version, extensions, custom code, infrastructure, and release process.

A Magento development company should make trade-offs explicit. We identify what can be retained, what needs modernization, and how to reduce risk before changing production commerce workflows.

Magento 2 services

  • Magento Open Source development: implement and extend the open-source Magento platform with maintainable code and clear upgrade considerations.
  • Custom module and extension development: build scoped business behavior, admin workflows, API integrations, and extension points without hiding operational impact.
  • Storefront and theme development: develop responsive storefront interfaces and refine existing themes around customer journeys, accessibility, and performance.
  • Magento upgrades and migrations: assess version compatibility, extension readiness, data and URL continuity, test coverage, and rollout steps before migration.
  • Performance optimization and scalability: investigate storefront and service performance, database and cache behavior, asset delivery, and workload bottlenecks; validate improvements with measurements.
  • Multi-store and international commerce: configure store views, catalog and locale requirements within the selected platform and operating model.
  • Security, maintenance, and ongoing support: plan dependency and patch work, regression checks, operational runbooks, and accountable maintenance boundaries.
  • ERP, CRM, PIM, and OMS integrations: map data ownership, synchronization direction, validation, retry behavior, and failure visibility for connected systems.

How an engagement is shaped

We begin with technical and business discovery: platform edition and version, deployment model, customizations, key integrations, peak operating needs, and release constraints. The resulting plan can focus on a contained improvement or a sequenced modernization program; scope and outcomes are agreed before implementation.

If the business needs Adobe Commerce-specific enterprise capabilities or a newer composable service, compare Adobe Commerce development, Commerce Optimizer, and Edge Delivery Services. For platform integrations across systems, discuss your Adobe Commerce project.

Further reading

Adobe Commerce Developer Documentation covers the supported PHP platform, APIs, storefront development, and extensibility. Platform-specific feature availability depends on the selected edition, version, deployment, and licensing.
