BigCommerce B2B Edition storefront, wholesale, distributor, and enterprise integration engineering scoped to confirmed product capabilities.

Discuss a BigCommerce B2B Project

BigCommerce B2B Edition implementation support

BigCommerce B2B Edition is intended for business-to-business commerce scenarios. 9Colon can help assess the project requirements, storefront and portal experience, data model, and integrations needed for a wholesale or distributor operation. The final scope depends on the current product capabilities, eligibility, and entitlements confirmed for the merchant.

B2B engineering areas

  • Wholesale and distributor portals: translate buyer and operator journeys into a clear storefront and account experience.
  • Business purchasing workflows: document roles, approval needs, catalog access, pricing inputs, and order processes for validation against the selected product.
  • Custom storefront development: align the portal experience with the merchant's design system and commerce APIs.
  • Enterprise integrations: coordinate catalog, account, order, and fulfillment data with ERP, CRM, PIM, OMS, and other systems.
  • Migration and modernization: assess existing B2B processes and preserve data, URLs, and operational continuity when changing platforms.

We validate feature availability and implementation details against current BigCommerce documentation rather than assuming the B2B product exposes the same capabilities or APIs as another edition. For broader storefront and application work see BigCommerce development. For 9Colon's primary commerce specialization see Magento & Adobe Commerce.
